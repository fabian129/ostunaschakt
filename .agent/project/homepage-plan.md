# Östunaschakt Homepage - Page Plan

## Business Goal
Establish Östunaschakt as the premium, reliable choice for groundworks in Uppsala/Stockholm. Drive contact inquiries ("Få offert").

## Target Audience
Construction managers, private homeowners, and developers looking for professional machinery and groundworks.

## Design Vibe
**"Industrial Precision"** — Dark, heavy, robust. Safety Red accents.

## Section Flow

### 1. Hero Section
- **Goal:** Immediate authority & capability demonstration.
- **Visual:** Background video (or high-res image) of excavator in action/fleet lineup. Dark overlay.
- **Content:**
  - Headline: "Komplett markleverantör i Uppsala" (H1, 700 weight)
  - Sub: "Schaktning, transport och material med precision."
  - Primary CTA: "Få offert" (Solid Red)
  - Secondary CTA: "Ring oss: [phone]" (Outline/Text)

### 2. Services Grid (Industrial Cards)
- **Goal:** Quickly communicate scope of offering.
- **Layout:** 2x2 or 4-col responsive grid.
- **Components:** `ServiceCard` (Dark surface, Red icon/accent, Hover lift).
- **Items:**
  1. Schaktning & markarbeten
  2. Transporter & leveranser
  3. Containerhyra
  4. Material (jord, grus, bergkross)

### 3. About / Why Us (Split Layout)
- **Goal:** Build trust through competence.
- **Layout:** 50/50 Split. Image Left (Driver/Team in gear), Text Right.
- **Content:**
  - "Kompetenta yrkeschaufförer"
  - "Bred fordonspark" (List machines)
  - "Lång erfarenhet"

### 4. Fleet Showcase (Visual/Interactive)
- **Goal:** Show, don't just tell. heavy metal appeal.
- **Component:** `FleetCarousel` or `ParallaxScroll`.
- **Content:** High-res images of: Lastväxlare, Budbilar, Grävmaskiner, Hjullastare, Kranbil.
- **Interaction:** Hovering a machine name reveals its image (or vice versa).

### 5. Verksamhetsområde (Map/Graphic)
- **Goal:** Confirm service area.
- **Visual:** Stylized map (Greyscale + Red pins).
- **Text:** Uppsala, Stockholm, Knivsta.

### 6. Customer Cases (Optional/Recommended)
- **Goal:** Social proof.
- **Component:** `BeforeAfterSlider` or simple "Project Card".
- **Items:** 2-3 examples (e.g., "Driveway Renovation", "Construction Site Prep").

### 7. Footer / CTA (High Contrast)
- **Goal:** Final catch for conversion.
- **Content:**
  - Headline: "Redo att starta?"
  - Button: "Kontakta oss"
  - Info: Address (Knivsta), Phone, Email, Org.nr.
  - Social Links.

## Validation
✓ **Hero** establishes "Heavy/Professional" feel immediately.
✓ **Services** are scanable.
✓ **Fleet** section uses valid "visual inspiration" (Industrial aesthetics).
✓ **CTA** is persistent and clear.
